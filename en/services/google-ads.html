Frequently Asked Questions

How much budget do I need to start with Google Ads?

Budget requirements depend on your geographic market and average CPC in your industry. We typically recommend a minimum ad spend of $500 to $1,500/month to collect sufficient conversion data for machine learning optimization.

What is Quality Score and why should I care?

Google Quality Score (rated 1 to 10) measures how relevant your keyword, ad copy, and landing page are to the searcher. Higher Quality Scores award you higher rankings at significantly discounted click costs.

Are Search Ads or Performance Max campaigns better?

Search Ads offer maximum precision control over keyword triggers, ideal for specialized services and B2B. Performance Max leverages Google AI across all properties simultaneously, ideal for e-commerce and broader scale.
